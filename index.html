How we teach

No fixed syllabus. Just your music.

Most academies hand every student the same graded lessons. We start with the person: where you come from, what you love to hear, what you want to be able to sing.

01

Built around you

Your background, your goals and your ear decide the path. A child who loves film songs, an adult returning to music, a grandparent who wants bhajans: each gets a plan of their own, reshaped as they grow.

02

Any music you love

One teacher, every tradition. Come with a Carnatic kriti, a Hindustani bandish, a Bollywood favourite or a Western song. Or come with curiosity and sample all of them.
