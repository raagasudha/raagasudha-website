What We Teach

Three traditions, one training ground

Hindustani

Raags, taals and voice culture, building toward the art of aalap and taan through a graded set of bandishes.

Carnatic

From foundational exercises to complex kritis and the improvisational freedom of manodharma.

Bollywood & Light Music

Your favourite film and light-classical tracks, sung with real pitch control, breath support and mic technique.
