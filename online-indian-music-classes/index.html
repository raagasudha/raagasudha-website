How online classes work

  1. Trial class on videoWe meet on a video call, hear your voice and talk about what you want to sing. You leave with a clear starting point.
  2. Live lessons, real feedbackEvery class is live — we sing, listen and correct in the moment, just as in a studio.
  3. Practice between classesShort practice plans and recordings so your riyaaz stays on track between lessons, including AI-assisted practice tools where they help.
  4. Switch to in person any timeIf you are in Singapore, you can move between online and in-person lessons at our Costa Rhu studio as life changes.

Students across time zones

Classes are taught from Singapore (SGT, UTC+8). Lesson times work comfortably for students in India, the UAE and the Gulf, Malaysia, Indonesia, Hong Kong and Australia. Families elsewhere can usually find an early-morning or evening slot — tell us your city when you enquire.

Frequently asked questions

Are online singing classes as effective as in-person classes?

For most students, yes. Lessons are live and interactive, so we hear and correct your singing in real time. Many students learn entirely online.

Which time zone are classes in?

Classes are taught from Singapore (UTC+8). Slots suit students in India, the UAE, Southeast Asia and Australia, and we can usually find early-morning or evening times for other regions.

Can children learn online?

Yes. Younger children do best with a parent nearby for the first few classes. Lessons are kept short and playful for ages 4–8.

Which styles are taught online?

All of them: Carnatic, Hindustani, Bollywood and light music, devotional bhajans and Western singing.

How do I pay for online classes from outside Singapore?

We accept international payments. Details, along with current pricing, are shared when you enquire.
