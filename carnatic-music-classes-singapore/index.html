What you will learn

Carnatic music is built on a clear progression of exercises and compositions. Each student moves through it at their own pace, and we spend longer wherever the voice needs it.

  1. Foundations — shruti, swara and talaSinging in tune with the tanpura, the seven swaras, and keeping tala with the hand. Sarali varisai, janta varisai and dhatu varisai build pitch, breath and evenness.
  2. Alankarams and geethamsThe seven alankarams across the suladi talas, then simple geethams that introduce ragas such as Malahari, Mohanam and Kalyani.
  3. Swarajathis and varnamsLonger compositions that train gamakas, speed and stamina — the bridge from exercises to concert repertoire.
  4. Kritis and bhajansCompositions of the great composers, devotional songs and the pieces you love, learnt with correct sahityam, bhava and gamaka.
  5. ManodharmaFor students who are ready: raga alapana, niraval and kalpana swaras — the improvisational freedom at the heart of Carnatic music.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior experience to join Carnatic classes?

No. Many of our students start from zero. The first lessons focus on listening, singing in tune with the tanpura and keeping tala, and we only move on when you are comfortable.

What is the right age for a child to start Carnatic music?

Children can start from around age 4 in our Little Voices program, which uses playful ear-training and rhythm games. Formal varisais usually begin once the child can sit for a short focused session, often around 6 or 7.

Are Carnatic classes available online?

Yes. Classes run live online for students anywhere in the world, and in person at our Costa Rhu studio in Singapore. You can move between online and in-person classes whenever your schedule changes.

Can my child prepare for Carnatic music exams?

Yes. Alongside regular lessons we offer an academic curriculum with structured guidance toward music certification and formal degree examinations.

How much do Carnatic classes cost?

Fees depend on the format you choose — one-to-one or group, online or in person. We share current pricing when you enquire, along with the next available trial slot.
